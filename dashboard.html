Method & caveats

• Signal computed on bar t close → position entered/exited at bar t+1 open (no lookahead bias).
• Fees + slippage charged on every fill; stop-loss fills at the stop price minus slippage when the bar's low breaches it (optimistic vs. gap-through risk).
• Sharpe/Sortino annualized with √(bars/year): 365 for 1d, 2190 for 4h, 8760 for 1h. Risk-free rate assumed 0.
• Long-only, 100% position sizing, one asset. No funding costs, no liquidation modeling, no partial fills, no maker rebates.
• Survivorship bias: you picked the coin knowing it survived. Backtest ≠ future performance.
